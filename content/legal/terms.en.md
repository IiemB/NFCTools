# Terms of Use

## About NFC Tools

NFC Tools lets you read, save, write, clone and emulate NFC tags with your iPhone. It uses parts of iOS that Apple doesn't offer to regular apps, so it only runs on iPhones set up for it, for example with TrollStore or a jailbreak. NFC Tools is an independent project. It is not made, approved or supported by Apple.

## Use It Responsibly

Only read, copy, write or emulate tags that you own or have permission to use. Copying or emulating access cards, transit cards, payment cards or other tags that belong to someone else may be illegal or against the rules of whoever issued them. You are responsible for how you use NFC Tools and for following the laws where you live.

## Your iPhone and Your Tags

While it works with NFC, the app may restart or take over your iPhone's NFC service, so Apple Pay, Wallet and other NFC features may not work until it stops. Writing to a tag can change or erase what's on it, and some changes, like locking a tag or changing its ID, can't be undone. Keep a copy of anything important first.

## NFC Tools Plus

NFC Tools Plus is an optional add-on for jailbroken iPhones that adds Control Center tiles and a command-line tool. These terms cover it too. Installing add-ons on a jailbroken iPhone is at your own risk.
