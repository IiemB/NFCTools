# Privacy Policy

## In Short

NFC Tools doesn't collect, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, ads or analytics, and the app sends nothing to the developer unless you choose to send a report.

## What Stays on Your iPhone

Tags you save (their ID, type, contents, nickname, icon and color), your settings, your widget and Control Center choices, and the app's activity log are kept only on this iPhone. Deleting a tag removes it.

## NFC Tags

When you scan a tag, your iPhone reads its data, and it's saved only if you choose to save it. Tag data is never uploaded.

## When Data Leaves Your iPhone

Only when you choose: for example, when you export a tag to a file and share it, or copy a value. Links in the app, like the developer's page, open in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.

To show the latest version of these terms, the app downloads them from GitHub. That request doesn't include any of your data, though GitHub can see your IP address, as with any website.

## Reports to the Developer

The Diagnostics page has a Send Report button. A report is sent only when you tap it, and you can see what it includes before sending. It contains the Diagnostics results, basic details like your iPhone model and iOS version, any note you add, and the app's activity log, which helps trace the problem and may include some details about tags you've used.

The log is encrypted on your iPhone before it's sent, so only the developer can read it; the services that deliver the report can't. Reports are only used to fix problems. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

## Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here. Questions? Contact the developer at github.com/IiemB.
